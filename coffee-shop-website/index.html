Our roast

We roast 8 kg at a time, four days a week.

Small batches mean we can taste every roast before it reaches the bar — and bin the ones that don't earn their place. Beans are bought direct from growers in Chikmagalur, Guji and Huila, and rested a full twelve hours before they ever meet a portafilter.
